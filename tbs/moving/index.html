Moving is more than a truck.

Moving is stressful when you have to coordinate packing, transport and logistics yourself. Clean Water Movers handles house moves, office relocations, and furniture or household goods transport across Nairobi, Kiambu, Githunguri and surrounding towns — so the move doesn't fall entirely on you.

Whether you're relocating a single apartment, a family home, or a full office, our crews work carefully with furniture, appliances and equipment from the first box to the final delivery.
