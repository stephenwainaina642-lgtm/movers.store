Clean water, delivered to your tank.

When your tank runs low, waiting is not an option. Clean Water Movers delivers water by tanker to homes, apartments, businesses, construction sites, schools, churches and other institutions across Nairobi, Kiambu, Githunguri and surrounding towns.
