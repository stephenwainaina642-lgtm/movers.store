House Moving in Nairobi

Moving apartments or a family home in Nairobi? We pack, load and transport your household goods carefully, from door to door.

Book Your House MoveCall 0720 792 185

Moving house in Nairobi involves more than a truck — it means packing, careful handling of furniture and appliances, navigating Nairobi traffic, and delivering everything safely to your new home. Clean Water Movers handles house moves of all sizes across Nairobi's estates and neighbourhoods.

We work with single apartments, family homes, and shared households, and can assist with packing if you'd prefer not to do it yourself.
